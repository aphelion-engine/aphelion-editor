# Code of Conduct

Aphelion welcomes participants of every background and experience level. Everyone
participating in this repository and its community spaces is expected to help
maintain a respectful, safe environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; criticize the work without attacking people.
- Respect different experiences, identities, accessibility needs, and viewpoints.
- Give useful context, accept good-faith feedback, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and use only material you have permission to share.
- Follow maintainers' moderation requests and keep discussion relevant.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information
without permission are not acceptable. Neither are repeated disruptive behavior,
spam, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
project-managed community spaces. It also applies when someone represents the
project publicly.

## Reporting a concern

For content on GitHub, use the offending comment's or account's **Report abuse**
action to report it privately to GitHub. GitHub's instructions are at
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

To contact project maintainers, use a private contact method a maintainer has
explicitly published on their GitHub profile. If no private maintainer contact is
available, use GitHub's reporting channel. Do not post sensitive incident details
or personal information in a public issue. Include links, dates, and the behavior
in question when reporting through a private channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, request a correction,
issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant for
serious or repeated violations. The response should reflect the severity,
impact, and pattern of behavior. A maintainer directly involved in a report
should leave review to another uninvolved maintainer where one is available.

Reports should be handled discreetly. Information should be shared only as
needed to investigate and respond. Participants may request reconsideration
through the same private channel, with relevant additional context.
